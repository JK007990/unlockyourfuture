Our Core Programs.

UYF develops programs across three primary areas designed to support education, youth development, and meaningful opportunities.

Focus Area 01

Education

Making learning more accessible for young people, particularly those facing barriers to traditional education.

  • GED Free Learning
  • Online Education
  • Accessible Learning Resources
Focus Area 02

Youth Development

Helping young people develop knowledge, skills, confidence, and leadership abilities.

  • Skills Development
  • Training Sessions
  • Leadership Programs
Focus Area 03

Opportunities

Connecting young people with opportunities that can support their personal and future development.

  • Scholarships
  • Volunteering
  • Career Development
